How It Works

A guided journey from self-reflection to career exploration. The assessment is designed as a starting point—not a final verdict on your future.

01

Complete the Assessment

Answer questions about your interests, working preferences, strengths and professional motivations.

02

Build Your Profile

Your responses are organised into a profile that highlights patterns across different dimensions.

03

Explore Career Matches

Review suggested career directions, understand why they may fit, and identify skills to develop.

Behind the experience

From responses to useful insight.

The project concept combines student-profile information with career descriptions to support structured comparison and exploration.

Methods in the project concept

Sentence-BERT
Represent text profiles and career descriptions semantically.

K-means clustering
Explore patterns and groups in profile data.

Cosine similarity
Compare profile and career representations.
