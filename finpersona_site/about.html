The idea behind FinPersona

About FinPersona

Choosing a career can feel overwhelming—especially when you are still discovering what you enjoy and where your strengths lie. FinPersona aims to make that exploration more approachable.

💬

Our purpose

To help students reflect on their own preferences and explore financial career options through an accessible, data-informed experience.

🤝

Our approach

We bring together self-assessment, profile representation and career information, while keeping the student in control of their decisions.

A helpful starting point

Not a label. A conversation with yourself.

A profile or match should never define what you can become. Use the results to generate questions, identify areas to develop and decide which opportunities you want to investigate further.
